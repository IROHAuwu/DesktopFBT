## Not bundled: pose models (downloaded on first use)

The pose models are **not** part of this package. DesktopFBT downloads them on first use from their official locations:

- RTMPose / YOLOX ONNX files (OpenMMLab): https://download.openmmlab.com/mmpose/v1/projects/rtmposev1/onnx_sdk/ (mirror: https://huggingface.co/Tau-J/RTMPose)
- RTMW3D-x: https://huggingface.co/Soykaf/RTMW3D-x

The license of the model weights and of their training data is **UNKNOWN** to this project; see the providers' pages. The open-source license of the related code (for example MMPose / rtmlib) does not by itself cover the weights.
